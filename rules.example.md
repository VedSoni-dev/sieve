# My rules

Write what you want and don't want, in plain words. Your agent reads this before every scroll.

## Hide
- Engagement bait ("like if you agree", "RT for…", rage-bait quote dunks)
- Giveaways, crypto shilling, "DM me for…"
- Ads and promoted posts

## Keep
- Anything from people I follow that has actual substance
- Long, thoughtful posts and threads
- Good photos

## Notes
- If you're unsure, keep it and tag it "unsure".
